# Data Analytics — Level 1 Task 1: EDA on Retail Sales Data
**Oasis Infobyte SIP** · Steven Manuel Rios

Notebook: [`retail_sales_eda.ipynb`](retail_sales_eda.ipynb), executed with all outputs embedded. The charts are in [`screenshots/`](screenshots/).

**Dataset:** Retail Sales Dataset (Kaggle, Mohammad Talib), 1,000 transactions from 2023, in `data/`.

**Key results:** Electronics, Clothing and Beauty earn almost equal revenue. May, October and December are the peak months and September the trough. Unit price drives revenue (r = 0.85), and basket size does not fall at higher prices. The notebook includes 4 business recommendations.
